Juicy Grilled Chicken Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 4 chicken thighs or breasts
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh herbs (optional)

Steps:

  1. Marinate chicken with all ingredients for 1–2 hours.
  2. Heat grill or grill pan on medium-high.
  3. Place chicken on grill, cook each side 6–8 minutes.
  4. Ensure inside is cooked well and outside is golden.
  5. Garnish with herbs and lemon slices.
  6. Serve hot with salad or rice!
