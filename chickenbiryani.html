Delicious Chicken Biryani Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500g chicken (cleaned and marinated)
  • 2 onions (sliced)
  • 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  • Ginger garlic paste, yogurt, mint, coriander
  • Spices: Biryani masala, turmeric, chilli powder, garam masala
  • Ghee, oil, salt

Steps:

  1. Soak rice and cook it till 70% done. Set aside.
  2. In a large pot, sauté onions in ghee/oil until golden.
  3. Add tomatoes, spices, and marinated chicken. Cook till tender.
  4. Layer rice and chicken. Sprinkle mint, coriander, saffron milk.
  5. Cover and cook on low heat (dum) for 20 minutes.
  6. Serve hot with raita or boiled egg!
