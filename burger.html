How to Make a Homemade Burger

Ingredients:

  • 2 burger buns
  • 1 beef/chicken/veggie patty
  • Cheese slices, lettuce, tomato, onion
  • Ketchup, mayonnaise, mustard

Steps:

  1. Toast the burger buns lightly on a pan.
  2. Grill or pan-fry the patty until cooked well.
  3. Place lettuce and patty on the bottom bun.
  4. Add cheese, tomato, onion, and sauces.
  5. Cover with the top bun and serve hot!
