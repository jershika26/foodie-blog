Spicy Bread Omelette Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 small onion (chopped)
  • Green chillies (chopped)
  • Salt, pepper, turmeric
  • Oil or butter

Steps:

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and beat well.
  2. Add onion, chillies, salt, pepper, turmeric and mix.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and pour the egg mixture.
  4. Place bread slices on top of the egg while it cooks.
  5. Flip carefully and cook both sides until golden.
  6. Serve hot with ketchup or chutney!
