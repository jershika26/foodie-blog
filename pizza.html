How to Make a Cheese Pizza

Ingredients:

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Oregano, basil

Steps:

  1. Roll out the dough on a tray.
  2. Spread tomato sauce evenly.
  3. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese generously.
  4. Bake in a preheated oven at 220°C for 12–15 minutes.
  5. Sprinkle herbs and slice to serve hot!
