Fluffy Homemade Pancakes

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 egg, 1 cup milk
  • 1 tsp baking powder, a pinch of salt

Steps:

  1. Mix flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Whisk in egg and milk until smooth.
  3. Heat a pan and lightly grease it.
  4. Pour batter and cook until bubbles form.
  5. Flip and cook other side. Serve with syrup!
