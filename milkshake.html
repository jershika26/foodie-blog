How to Make a Milkshake

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups of your favorite ice cream
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence (optional)
  • Toppings: whipped cream, chocolate chips, cherries, etc.

Steps:

  1. Add the ice cream and milk into a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth. Add more milk if needed for thinner texture.
  3. Pour into a glass and add toppings of your choice.
  4. Serve immediately and enjoy!
