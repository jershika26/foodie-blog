Spicy Chicken Shawarma Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 500g boneless chicken (thinly sliced)
  • 2 tbsp yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp garlic paste
  • 1 tsp paprika, cumin, coriander powder
  • Salt, pepper, olive oil
  • Pita bread, mayo, veggies for serving

Steps:

  1. Marinate chicken with yogurt, lemon, garlic, and spices.
  2. Let it rest for at least 1 hour (overnight best).
  3. Grill or cook chicken until nicely browned and cooked.
  4. Warm pita bread and spread garlic mayo.
  5. Add chicken, veggies (onion, lettuce, tomato), and roll.
  6. Wrap in foil and serve warm!
