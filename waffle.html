Golden Crispy Waffle Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 and 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps:

  1. Preheat your waffle iron.
  2. In a bowl, mix flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  3. In another bowl, whisk milk, eggs, melted butter, and vanilla.
  4. Combine wet and dry ingredients. Stir until smooth.
  5. Pour batter into waffle maker and cook until golden.
  6. Serve warm with honey, butter, or fruits!
